## 🌟 Key Features

- **Decision Tools Suite (`/tools`):**
  - **AI Eligibility & Scholarship Scorer:** 3-step wizard with dynamic partner university recommendation cards.
  - **Cost & 28-Day Bank Solvency Calculator:** Total budget estimator with live BDT currency conversions & embassy maintenance solver.
  - **Intake Timeline Planner:** Milestone tracker for September 2026, January 2027, and May 2026/2027 with print export.
  - **Interactive Visa Document Checklist:** Category-filtered compliance tracker with progress meter.
- **Advanced 4-Step Appointment Booking Engine:**
  - In-Person (Dhanmondi Head Office) vs Virtual VIP Session (Google Meet / WhatsApp Video).
  - Dedicated Counselor Selection (*A.S. Sajal - Founder & Lead Counselor*).
  - Interactive Date & Time Slot Picker with instant booking voucher (`ASE-2026-XXXX`).
- **Speed-Dial Floating Contact Hub:**
  - Real-time Facebook Messenger, Direct Voice Hotline, and Official WhatsApp floating actions.
- **Official Grounded Data:**
  - Authentic branch details, 0৳ file opening fee, UK 24h Super Priority, Australia Subclass 500 & Spouse Work Rights, Canada 3-Year PGWP, and European pathways.
